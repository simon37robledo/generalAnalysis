# New results after the 5-6 November rerun (old -> new)

What changed in the inputs (details in CHANGES_2026-11.md): (1) unit criterion = BombCell without "present during the
whole recording" (per-block presence kept); (2) grating onsets corrected (recordings 1 and 8 were one second early in
every trial, PV97/SA9 ~30/80 trials at the TTL). Old numbers: Paper_figs\Archive_standardBC_20261105.
"LOAO" = in how many leave-one-animal-out runs the effect stays significant in the same direction.

| metric | old | new |

Likely cause: in recordings 1 and 8 the old "static" window was actually the blank second *before* the grating, which
made static responses look absent and inflated drifting > static. The effect is still in the same direction and
borderline significant, but no longer robust to dropping animals.

## Figure 5 — direction selectivity
- MB: 0.35 [0.22, 0.50] of units direction-selective (225/477), p<0.001, all 8 LOAO (old 0.42, 117/253).
- SDGm: now above chance: 0.21 [0.06, 0.44], p=0.0096 (old 0.22, p=0.13 n.s.).
- MBR: 0.10, n.s. (old 0.17, n.s.).

## Figure 2 — spatial tuning (similar)
- Paired MB - SB tuning index: +0.46 [-0.04, 1.47], one-tailed p=0.037, 91 units / 5 animals (old +0.58, p=0.035, 77 units).
- Stripe RFs: MB 32/321 (10.0%) vs non-responsive baseline 12/261 (4.6%); SB 12/163 (7.4%) vs 7/551 (1.3%) (old: 11.2% vs 5.1%; 6.3% vs 1.4%).

## Figure 6 — depth (no effects)
- No depth relation for any stimulus. The single old BH survivor (MB stripe RF yes/no, p=0.0017) is gone (p=0.49).

## Figure 7 — Laudakia (as in the sensitivity run)
- MB vs SB (4 animals, 56 units): not significant (z +3.29 [-0.38, 4.92] p=0.40; delta +2.93 p=0.57; fraction p=0.72),
  but now pointing MB > SB (old: delta rate pointed the other way).
- Gratings (2 animals): drifting > static z p=0.014 (LOAO 1/2); MB vs SDGm n.s.; SB > SDGs delta rate p=0.0004 (2/2).
