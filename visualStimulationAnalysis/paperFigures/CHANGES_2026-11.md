## 2. Unit criterion changed — 5 Nov (your decision)
- **What**: a unit is "good" if it passes every BombCell quality check **except** "present during most of the whole
  recording"; it must still be present (>= 7 of 10 bins) during each stimulus block being compared — this second rule
  already existed.
- **Why**: some recordings last hours and the stimuli are only at the start, so units present during the stimuli but
  lost later were discarded. Good units 1063 -> 2602 in the 55 figure recordings.
- **Check done**: applying BombCell with all its rules to the saved metrics gave back exactly the old labels (55/55).
- **Old version**: `backup_standardBC_20261105` in each sorting folder; old per-recording results in
  `<stimulus>_Analysis\archive_standardBC_20261105`; old figures/tables in `Paper_figs\Archive_standardBC_20261105`.
- **Undo**: copy the three files from `backup_standardBC_20261105` back and move the archived caches back.

## 3. Grating timing corrected — 5 Nov
- **What**: grating trial onsets in every recording = trial TTL + measured delay to the static-grating onset (77-96 ms),

  trials of PV97 and SA9, and in trial 1 of PV35; and the old onsets of PV51/PV233 could not be regenerated by the code.
